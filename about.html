About Tsarmoire

A legacy of quality in women's garment manufacturing.

Our Story

Founded with a vision to bring Nepal's craftsmanship to the global stage, Tsarmoire Manufacturing has evolved into a premier B2B partner for international fashion brands. We combine traditional expertise with modern industrial efficiency.

Our facility is designed to handle diverse order volumes while maintaining strict quality control, ensuring that every piece that leaves our factory meets global export standards.
