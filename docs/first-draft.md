# Website Specification Document
## Women Wear Garment Manufacturing Website

---

## 1. Business Overview

**Business Type:** B2B Garment Manufacturer  
**Specialization:** Women Wear  
**Positioning:** Premium-Affordable + Sustainable Manufacturing  
**Target Markets:** Nepal, Europe, Australia  
**Business Model:** Private Label / Contract Manufacturing

---

## 2. Website Goal

The website is intended to:

- Attract international B2B buyers and fashion brands
- Showcase manufacturing capability and reliability
- Communicate sustainability and ethical production
- Generate RFQs (Request for Quotation)
- Build export credibility

---

## 3. Target Audience

- Fashion brands seeking production partners
- Importers / wholesalers
- Startups launching women wear labels
- Sustainable fashion companies

---

## 4. Brand Direction

**Tone:** Professional + Fashion-Oriented  
**Feel:** Clean, modern, export-grade  
**Balance:** Industrial capability + Fashion sensibility

---

## 5. Website Structure

### 5.1 Home Page

**Hero Section**
- Strong visual of production or finished garments
- Tagline: *Premium Sustainable Women Wear Manufacturing*
- CTA: Get a Quote / View Capabilities

**About Snapshot**
- Experience
- Capacity
- Sampling ability
- Export focus

**Product Categories**
- Dresses
- Tops & Blouses
- Co‑ord Sets
- Ethnic Wear
- Custom Designs

**Manufacturing Strength**
- Sampling Unit
- Cutting
- Stitching Lines
- Finishing
- Quality Control
- Packing

**Sustainability Section**
- Ethical workforce
- Responsible sourcing
- Waste reduction practices

**Why Choose Us**
- Premium finishing
- Competitive costing
- Flexible MOQ
- On-time delivery
- Export-ready documentation

**Inquiry Section**
- Buyer details form
- Tech pack upload option
- Quantity + timeline fields

---

### 5.2 About Us Page

- Company background
- Mission & values
- Manufacturing philosophy
- Market experience

---

### 5.3 Capabilities Page

Details of:
- Sampling development
- Pattern making
- Fabric sourcing support
- Production workflow
- Quality assurance system

---

### 5.4 Products Page

Category-based presentation.
Focus on:
- Types of garments produced
- Customization capability (not retail catalog)

---

### 5.5 Sustainability Page

Explain:
- Ethical production environment
- Compliance readiness
- Sustainable materials (if client-required)
- Waste-conscious manufacturing

---

### 5.6 Contact / RFQ Page

Form Fields:
- Company Name
- Contact Person
- Email / Phone
- Product Type
- Quantity
- Target Market
- Upload Tech Pack

---

---

## 7. Technical Recommendations

**Platform:** WordPress (recommended for flexibility & SEO)

**Must Have:**
- Mobile-first design
- Fast load speed
- SEO structure
- Inquiry tracking
- WhatsApp integration (optional)
- Easy future updates

---

## 8. Design Style Guidelines

- Neutral color palette (white / beige / grey)
- One accent color only
- Large imagery
- Minimal animation
- No clutter
- Focus on credibility

---

## 9. Phase 1 Deliverable

This version will be a **credibility website**, not a marketing-heavy one.

Goal = Trust + Capability + Inquiry Generation.

---

## 10. Future Phase (Not Now)

- Certifications display (once obtained)
- Case studies
- Virtual factory tour
- Buyer resources section
